About the event

Cozy Winter Sundays is a fan-organized birthday cupsleeve event celebrating Joshua Hong of SEVENTEEN, inspired by the warmth of Sunday mornings and cozy winter days.

When

One Sunday, four cities, celebrated at the same time by fellow CARATs.

Format

Come-and-go CSE

Drop by your host café any time during event hours. Some cities may add games and fan activities.

Event kit

Claim yours

Register, then meet your city’s minimum order at the host café to claim your event kit.

Raffle

Sponsored prizes

Raffle prizes and merchandise are shared across all four locations, depending on sponsor availability.

Pick your location

The celebration happens in four cities at the same time. Choose the one you’ll attend and you’ll be taken to its registration.
